# Dream Team Consulting

**Computational solutions to biological problems.**

Dream Team Consulting is the independent practice of [Tahreem Karim](https://www.tahreemkarim.xyz/science), a computational biologist who started at the bench. It works with researchers, labs, clinics and companies in healthcare, biotech and bioengineering, and ecology and conservation.

**[consulting.tahreemkarim.xyz](https://consulting.tahreemkarim.xyz)**

## What I do

- **Need-based tools:** small tools that fit the job, instead of a pipeline you have to learn first.
- **Information visualization:** figures, dashboards and maps that answer a real question. [Read more](https://consulting.tahreemkarim.xyz/infovis/).
- **Independent analysis review:** a careful second read of your methods, results and interpretation.
- **Ongoing research and decision support:** experimental design, statistics, machine learning and biological interpretation, as a project grows.
- **Academic and hospital lab support:** extra hands and a fresh eye for labs that run on small teams.

## Privacy and ethics

Privacy comes first. Client names, data and results stay private unless the client agrees otherwise, and I'm glad to sign an NDA before any confidential information is shared. Your data does not go into AI tools that store it or learn from it without your written agreement.

This website has no cookies, trackers or analytics, and loads nothing from third parties.
