Services

Help with one defined question or an ongoing project, for researchers, labs, clinics and companies working in healthcare, biotech and bioengineering, or ecology and conservation.

Large pipelines take time to adapt and learn. Often a person or a team needs one specific thing done well, and soon.

So I start by listening. Qualitative methods such as the KJ method (affinity mapping) sort what you need from what you were told you need, and I step in only where it helps. The goal is that you leave with less work than you came with, sometimes after a single meeting.

How a project works

Scoping review

A look at your question, your data, your current workflow and your limits. With your team, I run a short affinity-mapping session (the KJ method) to sort what needs doing from what doesn’t.

You receive

A short written summary: what’s feasible, what’s missing, what I recommend, and what I’d leave alone.

We agree the inputs, depth, timing and a fixed scope in advance. A small pilot can then test one approach, if that helps.

Ask about a scoping review

  1. Listen

    Your question, your team, your constraints and your timeline.

  2. Map and scope

    Needs mapped together, then a written scope, quote and milestones.

  3. Build and discuss

    The agreed work, regular updates, and findings explained as they come.

  4. Hand over

    Results, limits, documentation, and code your team can run without me.

How I think about AI

I use AI tools every day. They make coding and analysis faster and put them within reach of more people.

But output that looks right is not the same as output that is right. A model can run cleanly on the wrong question; a chart can be beautiful and misleading. So I check: is the comparison fair, are the controls enough, does the result hold up a second way, and does it make biological sense?

Two rules I keep. Your data does not go into AI tools that store it or learn from it without your written agreement. And I tell you where AI helped with the work. The judgment, and the responsibility, stay with me.
